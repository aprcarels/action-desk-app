# Action Desk — Revised Strategic Roadmap

## Product Vision
Action Desk is an **Operational Intelligence & Coordination Layer** that enhances existing operational systems and communication workflows.

## Core Strategic Goals
- Improve CSR operational efficiency
- Improve customer transparency
- Reduce operational friction
- Centralize operational awareness
- Provide actionable operational intelligence

## Product Philosophy
Action Desk does NOT replace:
- Outlook
- Teams
- Slack
- ERP systems
- WMS systems

Action Desk enhances them through:
- Operational intelligence
- Workflow coordination
- Risk visibility
- AI-assisted summaries
- Deterministic operational logic

## Phases
1. Operational Intelligence Foundation
2. Internal Command Center
3. Management Intelligence
4. Customer Experience Platform
5. Advanced Operational Intelligence
