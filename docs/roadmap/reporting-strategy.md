# Reporting Strategy

## Goal
Provide operational visibility, customer experience visibility, and executive intelligence.

## Reporting Layers

### Operational Health
Track:
- SLA performance
- Workflow aging
- Escalation volume
- Carrier performance
- Bottlenecks

### Customer Experience
Track:
- Response times
- Escalation frequency
- Repeat issue rate
- Proactive notification rate
- Customer effort indicators

### CSR Effectiveness
Focus on:
- Workload balancing
- Resolution speed
- Prevented escalations
- AI-assisted efficiency

## Executive Intelligence
AI-generated summaries:
- Daily operational summaries
- Weekly risk analysis
- Trend detection
- Predictive operational insight
