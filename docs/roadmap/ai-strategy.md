# AI Strategy

## Core Principle
Deterministic truth first.
AI interpretation second.

## Deterministic Responsibilities
Python/rules engines determine:
- SLA calculations
- Risk scoring
- Escalation triggers
- Workflow prioritization
- Aging
- Operational truth

## AI Responsibilities
AI handles:
- Summaries
- Recommendations
- Customer communication drafts
- Executive summaries
- Context interpretation

## AI Safety Principles
- No hallucinated operational facts
- Human approval for critical actions
- Confidence scoring required
- Audit logging for AI-generated actions

## Future AI Goals
- Predictive SLA risk
- Operational forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Intelligent workflow recommendations
